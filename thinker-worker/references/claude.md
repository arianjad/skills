# Claude Code routing

The installer generates one agent per role and tier from `routes.json` (`harnesses.claude.roles`): `~/.claude/agents/tw-<role>-<tier>.md`, with `model:` the role's first listed model and `effort: <tier>`. As shipped: `worker` on `opus` at `low`/`medium`/`high`/`xhigh`; `leaf` on `sonnet` at `low`/`medium`; `independent-review` and `ideation` on `fable` at `high`/`xhigh` (ideation also admits Opus ids). Invoke native `Agent` with `subagent_type: tw-<role>-<tier>`, the role matching the brief's `TW-Role:` line: the tier is the effort. Dispatch `tw-<role>-<tier>` with no `model` argument, except to select an allowed non-default model for the role (today: `model: opus` on `tw-ideation-<tier>`); the agent file pins the default. Fable is the ideation default; Opus is the coordinator's choice when the Fable weekly budget binds. Review stays Fable-only. A per-call `model` outside the role's `models` list is denied (`model <m> is not allowed for <role>`). A per-call model takes precedence over the agent file, so check the effective child model/effort in `/tasks` or supported runtime turn metadata; a request accepted by the guard is not that evidence.

A Sonnet leaf is for a short, objectively checkable task; prefer a deterministic command for exact extraction when sufficient. Do not assign it physics judgment, architecture, adversarial review, or acceptance. Keep work that may need to stop and escalate (physics conventions, ambiguous acceptance) on a worker.

Use the activation command in the rendered `SKILL.md`; that is where Claude substitutes the exact `${CLAUDE_SESSION_ID}` and `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}` values. Do not activate on cwd alone. The skill may be discovered during an existing session, but installed settings hooks or agent definitions may require a new session. `status` reports the activation record, not hook loading or effective routing. New session IDs start inactive; same-session resume stays active until `deactivate`.

The PreToolUse hook covers fresh native `Agent` calls only. It denies fork requests made through `Agent` (`fork`/`fork_context`) and resume fields, and does not cover `SendMessage` continuation, forks started any other way, arbitrary shell/provider paths, or model substitution by provider policy. Before continuing a child, verify its existing model/effort, role, and assignment. Everything up to admission fails closed (any guard error denies); the hook leaves ordinary permission checks intact on accepted calls. A user-pinned model or effort that conflicts with this mode must be surfaced, not overwritten.

**Routing.** After admission the hook computes a router decision and appends a `route` receipt row; a failure from here on writes a `kind: "error"` row (`where: "route"`) and the admit stands. The backends in `routes.json` `router.backends` run in order within `router.budget_s`; an exception, overrun, or invalid answer gives `source: coordinator` (the tier you dispatched), except that a ticket whose coin (`int(ticket, 16) / 16**12`) falls below the class's `explore` goes to the role's next tier below yours (`source: explore`, confidence 0; never from the role's cheapest tier). As shipped `router.backends` is empty and every class is `advisory` with `explore: 0.2`, so about 20 % of dispatches above the role's cheapest tier are advised one tier down and the rest run at your tier; `router.table` stays in the file, inert while no backend is listed. The mode per `TW-Class` comes from `router.classes` (`*` is the default): `shadow` logs; `advisory` denies with the router's pick and the hint to redispatch that agent or add `TW-Override: <reason>`; `active` allows the call with `subagent_type` rewritten to the pick (`updatedInput`). In `active`, the rewrite reaches the coordinator only as the hook's `additionalContext` line (`thinker-worker: dispatched as <router agent> instead of <requested agent> (router p=<confidence>); judge the result at that tier`); the child's real agent is its `meta.json` `agentType`, and the route row has `action: "rewrite"` and `router_agent`. Either action needs a different tier at confidence at least `router.cutoff`, or exploration (a lower tier, for tickets whose hash falls below the class's `explore` fraction). The router's pick is first raised to the highest `router.risk_floor` among the brief's `TW-Risk` flags (`physics` → `high`, `destructive` and `external` → `medium`); a pick raised to your tier or above takes no action and its row is `eligible: false`. The floor lifts only the router's pick: a dispatch of yours below a flag's floor is not advised up (physics never below `high` is your rule, in `SKILL.md`). A non-empty `TW-Override:` line in brief lines 2–12 keeps your tier. The ticket is a hash of the brief without its `TW-Route:` lines (a line the coordinator adds from `tw.py route` output, the phase-2 Codex v2 carrier) and `TW-Override:` lines; a re-dispatch of the same ticket in the session reuses the first backend or exploration decision (`source: cached:<backend>` or `cached:explore`, never re-explored), while coordinator decisions are recomputed.

**Exploration protocol.**
1. A denial saying `router picks <tier> (exploration)` means: re-dispatch the **same brief unchanged** at that agent. The same ticket reuses the cached decision, so it is admitted.
2. Judge the result as usual. Rejected because the tier was too low → `tw.py outcome … --accepted no --cause tier`, then re-dispatch the same brief unchanged at your original tier (admitted: cached, no re-exploration). Rejected for another reason → `--cause brief` or `--cause other`.
3. If the brief must change before a re-dispatch (at the explored tier or back at yours), add `TW-Override: exploration re-dispatch, brief changed` in lines 2–12. A changed brief is a new ticket, so without `TW-Override` a re-dispatch at t−1 can itself be explored down to t−2, and one at t can be explored again.
4. Keep your tier with `TW-Override: <reason>` only where exploration is unsafe for a reason the risk flags do not capture; an overridden dispatch the router would have explored shows on its route row as `eligible: true` with `action: null` (receipts never hold the override text).

**context-mode.** The home heal (`~/.claude/hooks/plugin-patch-heal.mjs`) removes context-mode's PreToolUse `Agent` hook at SessionStart, and a SubagentStart nudge replaces its prompt block (decision and rationale: `~/obsidian-vault/03-decisions/2026-09-26-effort-routing-single-writer-router-context-mode-coexistence.md`). The guard in `tw.py` (`competing_agent_writer`) still holds an `active` class to advisory, with `[active held: <reason>]` on the denial, whenever context-mode is not explicitly disabled and a PreToolUse matcher of its matches `Agent` (Claude Code matcher rules, legacy `Task` included), its `hooks.json` changed after this Claude process started (or that start is unknown), its state cannot be read, or a race was lost earlier in the session.

**Receipts** (`~/.thinker-worker/receipts/claude/<sha256(session)>.jsonl`) never hold the brief body; header values are cut at 256 characters.
- `dispatch`: the gate's `admit` or `deny`. A router-advised denial still shows `admit` here and `action: "advise"` on its `route` row, so count dispatches that ran by joining on the route row's `action`, never by `admit` alone.
- `route`: `coordinator_tier`, `router_tier`, `source`, `mode`, `action` (`null`, `advise`, `rewrite`), `guard`, `eligible` (a backend disagreed at the cutoff or explored lower, so the router would act in any mode; `null` if the action step failed), `explore` (the class ε at this dispatch, 0 on a cached row), `target_tier` (the pick after the risk floor; `router_tier` stays the raw pick), `router_agent` (names `target_tier`), `agent_model` (the per-call model, else the role's default), `ticket`, `provenance`, and backend `errors` if any.
- `race`: at each dispatch and at `outcome`, every unchecked rewrite whose child has spawned is checked. The child's `meta.json` `agentType` must equal `router_agent`; only when `agentType` is absent, its first prompt must not carry context-mode's block. A loss sets the session advisory. A failed check writes `race-error`.
- `outcome` and `cost`: `tw.py outcome` labels the dispatch (the last label wins; `cause` is `tier`, `brief` or `other` from `--cause`, allowed only with `--accepted no`, else null) and, once the child transcript exists, writes one `cost` row from it (token sums over the last usage row per `message.id`; `model` is the last non-`<synthetic>` assistant `message.model`, the model that ran; `advisor_calls` and `advisor_available` show a Fable advisor consult or offer), or `cost-error`.

`tw.py route --harness claude --role <r> --brief-file <f>` prints the decision for a brief at the role's default tier and writes no receipt. `tw.py promote --harness claude [--class <c>]` reads every receipt file and prints a verdict: the router arm is labeled, race-verified lower-tier rewrites plus re-dispatches that took an advised lower tier; the coordinator arm is labeled `eligible` dispatches a backend wanted lower that ran at the coordinator tier. With Beta posteriors, `promote` if P(router − coordinator ≥ −0.15) > 0.8, `demote` if < 0.2, else `hold` (defaults of `promote()` in `tw.py`). A dispatch whose cost row shows advisor calls leaves both arms (`n_advisor_excluded`). Under advisory exploration the coordinator arm holds only explored dispatches kept with `TW-Override`, a selected sample, so read `promote`'s counts, not its verdict, until the phase-2 control arm lands.
